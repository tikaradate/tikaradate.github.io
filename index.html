About

I am a Master's student in Informatics at the Federal University of Paraná (UFPR) in Brazil, where I work on string algorithms and compressed text indexes. My research focuses on repetitiveness measures in strings, particularly the relationship between combinatorial and compression-based measures.

Previously, I completed my undergraduate degree at UFPR, where I received the SBC Outstanding Student Award and participated in ICPC competitive programming both as a competitor and as a coach.

Research Interests

Stringology, compressed data structures, repetitiveness measures, combinatorics on words, succinct data structures.
